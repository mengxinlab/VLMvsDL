Highlights

- Benchmarked Gemini-family VLMs for CT lung-nodule malignancy on LUNA25.
- Visual-only Gemini showed moderate signal, with AUCs near 0.68-0.71.
- Clinical text raised Gemini F3 to AUC 0.739, consistent with metadata conditioning.
- Even metadata-assisted Gemini stayed significantly below 3-D DL (AUC 0.872).
- LNDb external validation preserved the visual-only VLM-DL performance gap.
